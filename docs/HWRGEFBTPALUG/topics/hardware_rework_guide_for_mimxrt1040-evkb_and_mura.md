# Hardware Rework Guide for MIMXRT1040-EVK and Murata M.2 Module

This section is a brief hardware rework guidance of the Edgefast Bluetooth PAL on the NXP i.MX MIMXRT1040-EVK board and the Murata’s 1XK, 1ZM or 2LL solution - direct M.2 connection to Embedded Artists EAR00385 \(1XK\), EAR00364 \(1ZM\) or EAR00500 \(2LL\) M.2 modules.

The hardware rework has two parts:

-   HCI UART rework
-   PCM interface rework
-   Wake pin rework
